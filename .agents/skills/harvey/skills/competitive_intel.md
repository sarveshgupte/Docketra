# Competitive Battle Cards: Docketra

Use these when a prospect mentions a competitor or asks "how are you different?"

## Market Positioning
Docketra is purpose-built for Indian CA, CS, and corporate compliance firms. Unlike generic US project management tools (ClickUp, Asana) or closed proprietary databases (Jamku), Docketra keeps 100% of client documents inside the firm's private Google Drive while providing turnkey Indian statutory blueprints (MCA, GST, 3CD) and frictionless client intake.

## Our Unique Strengths
- True BYOS Data Sovereignty: All files live in the firm's Google Drive under restricted `drive.file` scope. Zero proprietary vendor lock-in.
- Zero Client Login Required: Clients drop bank statements, PANs, and bills via 1-click links, or clerks bridge WhatsApp files directly to the docket.
- Turnkey Indian Compliance Checklists: Pre-loaded with Companies Act (AOC-4, MGT-7), DIR-3 KYC, GST, and Tax Audit Form 3CD with 44 clauses.
- Complete Maker-Checker Audit Trail: Junior trainees prepare; qualified partners review and approve with timestamped audit logs ready for ICAI/ICSI peer reviews.
- 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee with flat life-locked Pioneer pricing for founding firms.

## Common Alternatives (Non-Competitors)
What prospects do instead of buying a solution like ours:
- Excel spreadsheets + shared WhatsApp groups (the default in 85% of Indian firms)
- Scattered local PC desktop folders and pen drives
- Generic US Kanban tools (Trello, ClickUp, Notion)

## vs. Excel & WhatsApp

**How we win:** Automated statutory due-date tracking, centralized client memory, zero lost WhatsApp attachments, and maker-checker approval controls with instant Google Drive filing.

**Their weakness:** Spreadsheets have no memory or alerts; files get buried in chat threads; sensitive PANs and DSC PINs sit unencrypted on article clerks' personal phones; institutional knowledge vanishes when clerks leave.

**Migration angle:** Keep promoter clients texting on WhatsApp as usual, but use Docketra's 1-click intake and Excel importer to organize working papers cleanly into Google Drive in 5 minutes.

**When a prospect says "We manage everything fine on Excel and WhatsApp":**
"Most firms we speak with do too. The breaking point usually comes in October when an article clerk leaves right before MCA filings, or a partner spends 45 minutes searching WhatsApp for an unfiled board resolution. Docketra lets your clients stay on WhatsApp while giving your partners full visibility into due dates and reviews in your own Google Drive. Worth a 5-minute look?"

## vs. Jamku / Practice Management Portals

**How we win:** BYOS Google Drive data ownership (we never hold client files hostage), zero client login friction (promoters don't need to register or remember passwords), and modern, clutter-free 2026 UI.

**Their weakness:** Proprietary cloud database lock-in (if you leave, your files are stuck or require expensive export dumps); requires business promoters to create accounts and passwords on an external portal, which clients frequently resist.

**Migration angle:** Switch to Docketra to regain 100% custody of your firm's working papers inside your own Google Drive with zero client login friction.

**When a prospect says "We use Jamku":**
"Jamku is well known in the accounting space. The biggest difference CA and CS partners tell us when moving to Docketra is data sovereignty: with Docketra, 100% of your client files stay inside your firm's own Google Drive rather than an external database. If you ever cancel, you keep everything. Plus, your clients never have to register or remember portal passwords. Worth comparing the two?"

## vs. ClickUp / Asana / Notion / Trello

**How we win:** Purpose-built for Indian statutory compliance on Day 1. Pre-configured with MCA calendars, CIN lookup, 4-eye maker-checker sign-offs, and compliance checklists. Zero setup consulting required.

**Their weakness:** Generic US task managers with zero understanding of Indian statutory laws, MCA penalties (₹100/day), DIR-3 KYC, or ICAI peer review audit trail requirements. Requires months of painful custom template configuration.

**Migration angle:** Eliminate months of configuring custom fields and workflows on generic boards by switching to a system designed out of the box for Indian CAs and CSs.

**When a prospect says "We set up ClickUp or Notion":**
"Those are great general tools. The challenge CA and CS firms run into is that ClickUp doesn't understand Indian statutory deadlines, CINs, or 4-eye partner sign-offs. Setting up compliance workflows usually requires months of trial and error. Docketra is pre-loaded with MCA, GST, and 3CD checklists ready on Day 1. Mind if I share how a 5-partner firm uses it?"
