# Product Knowledge: Docketra — The Company Brain for Indian Professional Firms

## What We Sell
Docketra is a sovereign practice operations platform built specifically for Indian Company Secretaries (PCS), Chartered Accountants (CA), and corporate compliance firms. It connects directly to the firm's private Google Drive (under restricted `drive.file` scope) so 100% of confidential working papers remain in the firm's own custody. It orchestrates junior article clerks and senior partners with turnkey statutory compliance blueprints (Companies Act AOC-4/MGT-7, SEBI, GST, and Tax Audit Form 3CD with 44 clauses), a frictionless 1-click client document drop link, and partner maker-checker approval sign-offs.

## Core Value Proposition
Run your practice from your own Google Drive with zero client file leakage, turnkey statutory checklists for rotating trainees, and 100% partner review before portal filings.

## Key Benefits
- 100% Google Drive Data Sovereignty: All client working papers and balance sheets live in your firm's Google Drive. Docketra strictly only reads and accesses the folder it creates (restricted `drive.file` scope) with zero access to pre-existing private archives.
- Zero Client Login Friction: Clients never have to register or remember passwords. They drop bank statements, bills, and notices via a 1-click upload link, or staff forward WhatsApp files directly into the docket.
- Turnover Defense for Article Clerks: Turn rotating CA/CS trainees into productive preparers on Day 1 with pre-loaded statutory checklists for MCA Annual Filings, DIR-3 KYC, GST returns, and Tax Audit Form 3CD.
- Partner QC & Maker-Checker Oversight: Complete 4-eye verification before government portal submission, producing a clean, timestamped audit trail ready for ICAI and ICSI peer reviews.
- Zero Lock-in & 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: If you cancel, 100% of your organized folders and filings remain in your Drive permanently.
