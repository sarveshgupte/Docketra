# One-Page Business Plan: Docketra

### The Problem
Indian Chartered Accountants (CAs) and Company Secretaries (CSs) manage millions of statutory filings under severe regulatory deadlines (MCA, ROC, GST, Income Tax). Their operations run on chaos: clients habitually dump unorganized files and bank statements into WhatsApp threads, articled assistants (trainees) churn every two years requiring constant retraining, and senior partners review filings on unverified Excel sheets. Existing software (like Jamku) locks confidential client documents into centralized third-party servers, charges mandatory ₹3,000–₹15,000 setup fees, and lacks immutable maker-checker audit trails required under ICAI SQC-1 guidelines.

### The Customer
The 120,000+ practicing CA and CS firms in India. Primary wedge: 2-to-6 partner boutique practices (50–200 active corporate clients) running Google Workspace who require enterprise-grade Maker-Checker oversight without enterprise software complexity.

### The Offer & Price
* **The Sovereign Practice Transformation Stack**:
  * **Operational Sovereignty**: Client files remain permanently in the firm's own Google Drive under restricted `drive.file` scope. Live in-app cryptographic audit confirms 0 pre-existing files readable; Docketra servers never hold or index confidential client returns or financials.
  * **Peer-Review Defensibility**: ICAI SQC-1 compliant maker-checker audit trail. Every docket preparation, draft, note, and partner approval is logged with tamper-evident timestamps.
  * **Zero-Leakage Compliance**: Trainee Laptop Shield prevents article clerks and junior staff from downloading raw client files or bank statements to personal laptops. In-app viewer only.
  * **The Bonuses**: WhatsApp Forwarding Bridge (promoters keep texting on WhatsApp; clerks forward media directly into dockets with 1-click Google Drive sync), complementary client web drop links, 15+ turnkey statutory blueprints (MCA, ROC, GST, 3CD), 24-hour concierge client list migration from Excel, and 30-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee.
* **Pricing (Anti-Race-to-the-Bottom Structure)**:
  * *Proof of Sovereign Isolation Pilot*: **₹0 forever** (capped at 2 seats, 5 clients, 15 dockets/mo). Built for skeptical senior partners to verify zero-access boundary and trainee protection on real client work before paying. 1-click friction-free upgrade with zero data migration.
  * *Starter Tier (Pioneer 30)*: **₹1,499 / month** billed annually (₹17,990/yr) or ₹1,999/mo monthly for up to 5 seats and 50 clients. (Promotional: **₹12,990 / year locked for life** for the first 30 founding firms with zero setup fees).
  * *Growth Tier*: **₹1,832 / month** billed annually (₹21,990/yr) or ₹2,499/mo monthly for up to 12 seats and 150 clients with shared department queues (+₹1,200/seat/yr add-on).
  * *Pro Tier*: **₹4,999 / month** (₹59,990/yr) for up to 25 seats, multi-branch, and DSC routing workflows.
