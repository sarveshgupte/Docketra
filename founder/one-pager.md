# One-Page Business Plan: Docketra

### The Problem
Indian Chartered Accountants (CAs) and Company Secretaries (CSs) manage millions of statutory filings under severe regulatory deadlines (MCA, ROC, GST, Income Tax). Their operations run on chaos: clients habitually dump unorganized files and bank statements into WhatsApp threads, articled assistants (trainees) churn every two years requiring constant retraining, and senior partners review filings on unverified Excel sheets. Existing software (like Jamku) locks confidential client documents into centralized third-party servers, charges mandatory ₹3,000–₹15,000 setup fees, and lacks immutable maker-checker audit trails required under ICAI SQC-1 guidelines.

### The Customer
The 120,000+ practicing CA and CS firms in India. Primary wedge: 2-to-6 partner boutique practices (50–200 active corporate clients) running Google Workspace who require enterprise-grade Maker-Checker oversight without enterprise software complexity.

### The Offer & Price
* **The Sovereign Practice Transformation Stack**:
  * **Sovereign Google Drive Custody**: Dedicated folder isolation under Google restricted `drive.file` scope; Docketra strictly only reads and manages the specific folder it created; 100% of client documents live in the firm's own Drive; Docketra cannot access or index pre-existing private files.
  * **Turnkey Maker-Checker Execution**: Junior clerks prepare statutory checklists; qualified partners review and sign off with DSC tokens.
  * **The Bonuses**: Direct WhatsApp Forwarding & Intake Bridge (promoters keep texting files on WhatsApp; clerks forward media straight into dockets with 1-click Google Drive sync), complementary 1-click client drop links, 15+ pre-loaded statutory blueprints (MCA, ROC, GST, 3CD), 24-hour concierge client list migration from Excel, and a 30-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee.
* **Pricing**:
  * *Free Sandbox*: ₹0 forever (capped at 5 active clients, 15 dockets/mo) for evaluation.
  * *Starter Tier*: **₹1,499 / month** billed annually (₹17,990/yr) or ₹1,999/mo monthly for up to 5 seats and 50 clients. (Promotional: **₹12,990 / year locked for life** for the first 30 founding firms).
  * *Growth Tier*: **₹1,832 / month** billed annually (₹21,990/yr) or ₹2,499/mo monthly for up to 12 seats and 150 clients with department queues (+₹1,200/seat/yr add-on).
  * *Pro Tier*: **₹4,999 / month** (₹59,990/yr) for up to 25 seats, multi-branch, and DSC routing workflows.

### The Evidence
* **Consumer Panel (Seed 42)**: Tested across 20 diverse Indian CA/CS personas (3 buy · 17 pass, 15% buy rate). Achieved 100% conversion in tech-forward practices. Identified the two key adoption blockers: a 4.6x price cliff for mid-sized firms (6–12 staff) and the need for a direct WhatsApp media forwarding flow for diehard clients.
* **Competitor Gap**: Jamku charges ₹6,500/yr + ₹3,000–₹15,000 mandatory setup fees on centralized servers; Zoho Practice lacks MCA/ROC corporate secretarial workflows. Docketra owns the uncontested intersection of *Sovereign BYOS + Zero Setup Fees + Turnkey Statutory Blueprints*.
