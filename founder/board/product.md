# Product lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The Sovereign BYOS Insight**: Eliminating data custody paranoia while dropping marginal hosting storage costs to zero is an elegant product decision. For professional practitioners bound by client confidentiality and regulatory scrutiny, leaving files inside their own Google Drive or AWS S3 removes the single biggest psychological barrier to cloud adoption.
- **The Maker-Checker Core Primitive**: Indian CA and CS firms operate under constant anxiety regarding regulatory penalties caused by junior staff oversight. Elevating quality control (a structured maker-checker review stage) into the central execution primitive directly addresses an acute operational pain that generic tools like Trello and Asana completely ignore.
- **Unified Chain of Custody**: Tying intake, working documents, review approvals, and an audit trail into a single workflow eliminates the fragmented, error-prone scramble across WhatsApp chats, email attachments, and local desktop folders.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The Feature-Sprawl Trap (Four Mediocre Tools Instead of One Masterpiece)**:
   Docketra is trying to be a CMS (intake forms), CRM (pipeline), Task Manager (dockets), and Storage Orchestrator (BYOS) simultaneously. Compliance firms do not need an unwieldy all-in-one suite. On a bootstrapped engineering budget, attempting to build four software categories guarantees that each will feel half-baked compared to dedicated alternatives. If the core docket execution feels cluttered or slow because engineering focus was diluted across CRM pipelines, users will churn back to Excel.
2. **The "Seam" in Onboarding: BYOS Technical Friction**:
   The first five minutes dictate product survival. If setting up BYOS requires a non-technical senior partner or office manager to configure AWS IAM policies, S3 bucket permissions, CORS rules, or Google Cloud console service accounts, onboarding will hit an immediate wall. Furthermore, if a partner clicks an attached tax filing and encounters a broken link or a cloud permissions error (403 Access Denied), trust in the platform is permanently destroyed.
3. **The Workflow Disconnect: The Structured Intake Fantasy**:
   The product assumes clients will obediently submit information through structured web intake forms. In reality, clients of Indian accounting firms dump disorganized PAN cards, bank statements, and GST invoices over WhatsApp and forwarded emails. If Docketra requires clients to adopt a formal portal before work can begin, article clerks will bypass the system to avoid double data entry. The product will break at the intake seam.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. What is the single interaction in Docketra that feels like pure magic—the one screen that convinces a 50-year-old senior partner to abandon WhatsApp and spreadsheets forever?
2. What does the first 90 seconds after sign-up look like? Can an article clerk connect their Google Drive and assign their first reviewable docket without consulting a guide or touching technical settings?
3. What are you willing to say "no" to today? If cutting the CRM pipeline entirely makes the Maker-Checker Docket 10x faster and clearer, why hasn't that been done?
4. How does the partner review work on mobile? Can a partner review an ROC filing annexure, inspect the clerk's notes, and approve or reject in under 30 seconds between client meetings?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Cut the CRM Pipeline and Focus on Statutory Recurrence**: Remove generic sales funnels and deal stages. Replace them with a Client Registry and a Statutory Compliance Calendar. Compliance practices do not manage sales pipelines; they manage relentless statutory deadlines (monthly GST, quarterly TDS, annual ROC). Every ounce of product energy must serve that cadence.
2. **Zero-Jargon, One-Click Storage Onboarding**: Reduce BYOS to a single-click Google Workspace / Drive OAuth flow that creates the firm's folder structure automatically in the background. Hide AWS S3 behind an advanced enterprise drawer. The firm must be ready to work within two minutes of account creation without seeing a single technical cloud term.
3. **Craft an Uncompromising 30-Second Partner Review Screen**: Perfect the maker-checker interface above all else. A senior partner needs a frictionless inbox showing pending approvals, side-by-side document previews without manual downloads, and one-tap approve/reject controls with quick voice or text feedback. If the senior partner does not find this screen effortless, the firm will abandon Docketra.
4. **Bridge the WhatsApp Ingestion Reality**: Rather than expecting SME clients to fill out structured CMS portals, build a frictionless ingestion seam (such as a dedicated firm email intake address or direct WhatsApp document drop) that lands unstructured client documents directly into a clerk's docket inbox for triage.
