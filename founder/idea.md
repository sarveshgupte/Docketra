# Docketra

- What it is: A sovereign practice operations platform that organizes client document collection into audit-ready dockets with automated Maker-Checker review sign-offs and pre-loaded statutory blueprints (Companies Act, MCA, GST, Tax Audit Form 3CD), storing 100% of client records inside the firm's own Google Drive.
- Who it is for: Practicing Chartered Accountants (CAs), Company Secretaries (CSs), Tax Consultants, and corporate compliance firms in India (120,000+ practicing firms).
- What it sells, at what price:
  - Free Sandbox (Proof of Sovereign Isolation): ₹0 forever for up to 2 team members, capped at 5 active clients and 15 monthly dockets (full feature parity: 1-click Google Drive BYOS with restricted `drive.file` scope, Trainee Laptop Shield, Maker-Checker QC, statutory calendar, audit log).
  - Starter Tier (Pioneer 30): ₹1,499/month billed annually (₹17,990/year) or ₹1,999/month monthly, for up to 5 team members and 50 active clients with unlimited dockets. (Promotional: ₹12,990/year locked for life for first 30 founding firms).
  - Growth Tier: ₹1,832/month billed annually (₹21,990/year flat) or ₹2,499/month monthly, for up to 12 team members, 150 active clients, and shared department queues (Tax, ROC, Audit), with extra seats at +₹1,200/seat/year.
  - Pro Tier: ₹4,999/month billed annually (₹59,990/year) or ₹5,999/month monthly, for up to 25 team members, unlimited clients, multi-branch, and DSC routing workflows.
  - 100% feature parity across all tiers; gating is by practice volume (active clients) and team seats.
- Where and how: Cloud SaaS web application (`docketra.in`). Customers find it via professional networks (ICAI/ICSI chapter seminars, study circle masterclasses, peer referrals, LinkedIn). Customer records stay in firm custody via Google Drive BYOS.
- Budget and constraints: Bootstrapped, engineering-led team. Tech stack: React (Vite) + Node.js/Express + MongoDB + Redis. Variable cost is ~₹150/firm-month (90% gross contribution margin). Fixed overhead: ₹85,000/month. Break-even at 63 active firms.
